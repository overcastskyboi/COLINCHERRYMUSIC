\# MISSION: Build the "thecolincherry.com" Music Artist Hub



\## 1. Core Pages (Strictly Music)

\- \*\*Home:\*\* Minimalist, high-impact hero section with "COLIN CHERRY" branding.

\- \*\*Music/Discography:\*\* A clean grid featuring album artwork and Spotify/Apple Music player embeds.

\- \*\*Videos:\*\* A dedicated section for YouTube music video embeds.

\- \*\*EPK / About:\*\* A professional bio section, high-res press photos, and contact info for bookings.



\## 2. Global Features \& Aesthetic

\- \*\*Theme:\*\* Ultra-dark (#0a0a0a) with "Glassmorphism" (blurred) navigation and cards.

\- \*\*Vibe:\*\* Emo-Rap / Alt-Pop aesthetic. No corporate or retail elements.

\- \*\*Navigation:\*\* Persistent, blurred header with links: \[Home, Music, Videos, Contact].

\- \*\*Mobile First:\*\* Must look like a high-end native app on mobile.



\## 3. Tech Stack

\- Vite + React + Tailwind CSS

\- Framer Motion (for smooth, atmospheric page transitions)

\- Lucide-React (for clean, thin-line icons)
